**METHODOLOGY (A Two-Step Process):**

**Step 1: Chain of Thought - Holistic Analysis**
First, you MUST write a comprehensive analytical paragraph. This is your "Chain of Thought" and the most important part of your task. In this paragraph, you must:
- Synthesize the overall connection between the event and the company's risks as described in the text.
- Discuss the nature of the risk: Is it a direct, existing impact, or a potential, forward-looking risk? Are the statements specific or generic?
- Explain your overall assessment of the risk level, explicitly considering the Time Decay Principle. This narrative forms the basis for your evidence scoring.

**Step 2: Evidence Extraction and Micro-Scoring**
After your holistic analysis, MUST extract **ALL** relevant sentences. For each sentence (`snippet`), you must create a `score_calculation` by breaking down your reasoning into individual `components`.

**Micro-Scorecard Rules for each `snippet`:**
1.  **Identify Components:** Deconstruct the sentence into its core informational parts. Each part becomes a `component`.
2.  **Assign Points:** Assign positive `points` for aggravating factors (elements that increase risk) and negative `points` for mitigating factors (elements that decrease risk).
3.  **Justify Points:** For each component, provide a `reason` for the points assigned.
4.  **Calculate Snippet Score:** The `final_snippet_score` is the sum of all component points. This score must still adhere to the **TIME DECAY PRINCIPLE**.

---
**CORE PRINCIPLE: THE TIME DECAY PRINCIPLE**
This principle MUST constrain your final `final_snippet_score`.
* **Pre-Event Rule:** If the `Filing Date` is BEFORE the `Event Timeframe` begins, the event has not yet occurred. In this case, the score for any evidence **MUST be 0**.
* **Recent Events (0-3 years):** Score can be high (e.g., 7-10).
* **Medium-Term Events (4-10 years):** Score should be moderate (e.g., 4-6).
* **Distant Events (>10 years):** Score **MUST NOT EXCEED 3**.
